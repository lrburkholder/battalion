### ADR-0008 — Engineering Knowledge System

#### Context

Engineering sessions routinely produce valuable observations.

Historically these observations were captured manually through Regiment's Learner
process.

Battalion names the equivalent knowledge-capture responsibility **Recon**.
"Learner" refers only to the historical Regiment process and is not a Battalion
node name.

Battalion requires a persistent mechanism for accumulating engineering knowledge
while preserving human authority over institutional knowledge.

---

#### Decision

Introduce the Engineering Knowledge System (EKS).

The EKS consists of four independent responsibilities.

Recon

Produces candidate instincts from completed executions.

Intel Repository

Stores immutable accepted instincts.

Intel

Selects relevant instincts for future node executions.

Operator Feedback

Captures usefulness feedback from the human operator and updates retrieval
confidence.

These responsibilities remain separate to preserve single responsibility and
allow independent evolution.

---

#### Consequences

Positive

- Engineering knowledge compounds over time.
- Prompt context remains focused.
- Human oversight is preserved.
- Retrieval quality improves through operational feedback.

Negative

- Additional persistence requirements.
- Retrieval subsystem complexity.
- Requires instinct indexing.

---

#### Deferred

Out of scope.

- Automatic standards generation.
- Automatic philosophy updates.
- Cross-project knowledge sharing.
- Semantic retrieval.
- Embedding-based ranking.
