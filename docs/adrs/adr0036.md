# ADR-0036 — Keep human workflow admission separate from evidence and execution

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-29  
**Implements:** BTN-141  
**Related:** RFC-0012, ADR-0026, ADR-0028, ADR-0032, ADR-0033, ADR-0034

## Context

RFC-0012 makes the human—not deterministic policy or Tactician—the
consequential decision-maker for a workflow admission. The decision must use
existing Actor/application authority rather than presentation checks, preserve
the independent assessment records, and fail closed when the supplied evidence
or policy has changed.

The implementation decomposition assigns compact execution and upgrade policy
to BTN-142, then durable admission/Run linkage and resume semantics to BTN-143.
Human admission must therefore not create a second execution path or a competing
persistence format.

## Decision

Battalion exposes transport-neutral application operations to inspect a current
deterministic admission assessment plus optional Tactician assessment and to
make one of four bounded human dispositions: full, compact, clarification, or
cancellation. Every operation re-evaluates supplied revision-pinned evidence
against the current deterministic policy; a mismatch is a typed stale-admission
failure requiring reassessment.

An active human Actor is resolved through the existing application authority
boundary before a decision is created. Full remains selectable by that Actor in
every outcome. Compact requires that same explicit human action, is prohibited
when deterministic admission is full-required, and, for uncertain work,
requires a Tactician assessment before it may be selected. Tactician remains
advisory: a human may choose full despite a compact recommendation or compact
despite a different valid recommendation.

The immutable decision contract records the exact selected recipe/version when
execution is chosen, deterministic assessment identity, optional Tactician
assessment identity, approving Actor, disposition, revisions, policy identity,
risk flags, timestamp, and a bounded optional annotation. It references rather
than rewrites the independently inspectable assessments.

## Consequences

BTN-141 does not dispatch a graph, register a compact recipe, mutate `RunState`,
or persist a decision. BTN-142 owns executable compact semantics and the
upgrade-only ratchet. BTN-143 will durably store the three separate records and
their Run/resume linkage. Presentation clients in BTN-144 consume these same
application contracts rather than embedding workflow policy or Actor checks.
