# ADR-0033 — Classify workflow admission from bounded deterministic evidence

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Implements:** BTN-139  
**Related:** RFC-0012, ADR-0003, ADR-0014, ADR-0032

## Context

RFC-0012 requires Battalion to avoid treating task complexity as a numeric
score or paying for a Tactician model call when bounded, authoritative evidence
already establishes the admission outcome.  The assessment must stay
inspectable, stable for identical inputs, and outside graph, presentation, and
provider authority.

Cartography evidence is an optional bounded input.  It may be unavailable,
stale, or contradictory; the admission policy must preserve that uncertainty
rather than infer compact eligibility from absence of a prohibition.

## Decision

Battalion owns a versioned `WorkflowAdmissionPolicy` and emits an immutable
`WorkflowAdmissionAssessment` from revision-pinned evidence identities.  The
assessment records the work-item and optional specification revisions, policy
identity/version, evidence references, outcome, reasons, observed hard-risk
flags, missing positive evidence, and admissible recipe identities.

The policy returns `FULL_REQUIRED` when a configured hard-risk flag is present.
The built-in configured surfaces are material architecture boundaries, public
interfaces/schemas, persistence/migrations, authorization/secrets/privacy/
security, high-consequence releases/deployments, and cross-system policy.
Projects may configure additional stable full-only flags.  A model cannot alter
the policy or override a configured hard prohibition.

`COMPACT_ADMISSIBLE` requires present, authoritative, non-mechanical evidence
for explicit desired behavior, applicable existing architecture, bounded scope,
executable behavioral verification, deterministic gates, and independent
review.  Mechanical signals may support evidence but cannot establish a compact
requirement on their own.  Missing, stale, contradictory, unconfigured-risk, or
otherwise insufficient evidence yields `UNCERTAIN`, except that a known
configured hard prohibition fails closed to `FULL_REQUIRED`.

The application boundary exposes policy inspection and assessment creation only.
BTN-140 owns Tactician assessment, BTN-141 owns human authorization, BTN-142
owns the compact recipe, and BTN-143 owns durable admission persistence.

## Consequences

Admission classification has no provider credentials, filesystem access, model
execution, graph construction, or persisted Run mutation.  Its content-derived
assessment identity makes identical evidence and policy inputs reproducible.
The policy can return a future compact recipe identity without registering or
dispatching that recipe; BTN-142 remains responsible for that execution policy.
