# ADR-0039 — Persist exact workflow admission separately from execution history

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-09-01  
**Implements:** BTN-143  
**Related:** RFC-0012, ADR-0014, ADR-0019, ADR-0020, ADR-0032, ADR-0033, ADR-0034, ADR-0036, ADR-0037

## Context

Workflow admission already produces three different facts: a deterministic
assessment, optional advisory Tactician evidence, and an authorized human
decision. Compact execution additionally has selected-recipe state and an
upgrade-only ratchet. A restart must retain those exact facts without treating
pre-admission assessment as a model-node attempt, re-running Tactician, or
selecting whatever recipe or policy happens to be current.

Legacy Runs predate workflow admission and remain historical evidence. Unknown
or corrupt recipe/admission state must not be interpreted by guessing a
default. At the same time, policy and registry changes must not erase the
meaning of a completed historical Run.

## Decision

`RunState` schema `1.1` may contain one versioned
`WorkflowAdmissionRunRecord`. The record retains four independently typed
parts:

1. the complete, independently versioned deterministic
   `WorkflowAdmissionAssessment`;
2. the optional complete `TacticianAssessment`;
3. the immutable, Actor-attributed `WorkflowAdmissionDecision`; and
4. the exact `WorkflowExecutionState`, including completed evidence and the
   append-only upgrade record.

Cross-record validation requires matching assessment, Tactician, revision,
policy, risk, decision, and exact recipe identities. This linkage does not turn
either assessment into a Run or node execution.

The shared application boundary authorizes the decision, creates the canonical
UUID Run, initializes the exact registered recipe, catalogs the Run, and saves
the baseline before execution. Application operations—not presentation
clients—also save stage, completion, and upgrade transitions. A completed Run's
admission/execution evidence cannot be mutated.

Resume resolves the persisted exact recipe and any full-workflow continuation.
An ordinary process restart uses the retained record and does not invoke
Tactician or re-admit the work. The existing v1 graph may resume only an
originally admitted full recipe; compact or upgraded state is retained for its
recipe-specific execution/stronger-handling boundary and never misrouted
through that graph. When an adapter supplies refreshed authoritative
evidence, it must reproduce the persisted assessment under the same policy; a
material difference or policy-version change fails with an explicit stale
admission requiring re-admission or stronger handling. Unknown recipe versions
remain inspectable historical records but cannot resume. A compact record is
never sent through the existing full-workflow graph merely because no compact
dispatcher is available.

Historical inspection does not require the original admission policy to remain
current. Exact retained records remain visible after policy/model changes, with
unknown recipe availability represented explicitly rather than rewriting the
original semantics.

Runs without `workflow_admission` are an explicit legacy compatibility case.
They remain readable under schema `1.0`; Battalion does not fabricate an
assessment, decision, or selected recipe for them.

## Consequences

Restart and worker supervision preserve exact human/model/policy evidence while
keeping human authority and Tactician advice separate. The registry must retain
historical recipe versions for resumability; removing one degrades the Run to
inspectable, non-resumable history rather than silently substituting another
version.

CLI and desktop presentation remain BTN-144 work. Recipe-specific execution is
not invented here: until a registered compact execution adapter exists, the
application fails closed instead of routing compact state through the v1 full
graph. Legacy Runs remain available but cannot claim admission evidence they
never recorded.
