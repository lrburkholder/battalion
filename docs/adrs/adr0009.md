### ADR-0009 — Confidence Represents Operational Usefulness

#### Context

Instincts require ranking.

Confidence may represent either subjective trust or observed usefulness.

---

#### Decision

Confidence represents observed operational usefulness.

Confidence SHALL be derived from operator feedback.

Confidence SHALL NOT be edited directly.

Confidence SHALL NOT determine whether an instinct is active.

Status and confidence are separate concepts.

---

#### Rationale

Operational usefulness reflects real engineering experience.

Separating status from confidence allows an instinct to remain active despite
limited evidence while preventing superseded instincts from remaining highly
ranked.

---

#### Consequences

Positive

- Ranking improves naturally.
- Human feedback directly improves retrieval.
- Confidence remains objective.

Negative

- Requires usage statistics.
