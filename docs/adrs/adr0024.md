# ADR-0024 — Keep Inference Identity and Cost Policy in Battalion

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-08-20

## Implementation status

Accepted by the human operator through BTN-51 on 2026-08-20. No runtime behavior
changes until delivery through BTN-52 through BTN-55.

BTN-52 branch implementation delivers the configured-target subset through
`NodeLLMConfig`: the existing LiteLLM `model` remains the requested transport
identifier, with separate endpoint URL, backend, location, family, and credential
reference fields. Setup and runtime use one request-parameter boundary. Legacy
plain models remain compatible, and legacy `extra_params.api_base` migrates to
the endpoint field. Endpoint-configured Driver/Reviewer roles require distinct
family declarations; opaque routes and equal requested identities are rejected.
These are operator configuration assertions, not fabricated resolution or
classification evidence. Runtime provenance/contradictions and verified
cost/locality admission remain BTN-54 and BTN-55. No new interrupt, role,
fallback authority, or graph transition is introduced by BTN-52.

## Context

Battalion uses LiteLLM model strings for per-role inference and compares the
configured Driver and Reviewer strings for diversity. That is sufficient for
direct, concrete targets but not for local OpenAI-compatible servers or remote
routers. An endpoint on localhost can route remotely, two aliases can resolve
to the same model family, and a provider can omit monetary evidence.

BTN-35 already distinguishes known zero from unknown monetary cost. Local and
free-tier inference must extend that transparency rather than infer identity or
authorization from a URL, display string, or infrastructure claim.

## Decision

Keep LiteLLM as the sole model-access boundary and make Battalion the owner of
inference identity, diversity, and cost policy.

Represent a configured target with separate requested model, provider,
backend, non-secret endpoint, inference-location classification, cost
classification, canonical model family, and sourced classification evidence.
Record resolved model and routed provider/model evidence when reliably
available; do not fabricate missing evidence or reconstruct typed identity from
display strings.

Provide three policies:

- `local-only` admits only verified same-host inference.
- `free-only` admits verified local or current verified-free targets.
- `paid-capable` admits explicitly configured paid, free, or unknown targets
  and is the backwards-compatible default.

Unknown classification fails closed for both zero-cost policies. Runtime cost
remains BTN-35 evidence: an admitted free call with no monetary response is
still unknown, not zero. A reported non-zero amount under a zero-cost policy is
recorded before pausing.

Enforce BTN-14 on distinct canonical model families. Provider, endpoint,
quantization, or alias differences alone do not satisfy diversity. Opaque
router aliases, profiles, smart routes, and fusion cannot serve Driver or
Reviewer unless their family can be constrained and proven before use. A
runtime contradiction invalidates the affected output or verdict and pauses at
infrastructure interrupt condition 5.

Retries and failover stay within the configured canonical family, inference
location, and cost-policy envelope. Credentials remain in the approved secret
boundary. Endpoint URLs and non-secret classifications may be persisted.

Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM, other OpenAI-compatible services, and FreeLLMAPI are
optional inference sources behind LiteLLM. None owns Battalion's roles, graph,
model selection, diversity, cost, or failure policy. A local proxy process does
not prove local inference, and a free-tier catalog does not prove durable zero
cost or production suitability.

## Consequences

- Existing plain configurations remain usable under `paid-capable`.
- Endpoint-aware configuration and richer durable evidence require explicit
  compatibility paths in BTN-52 and BTN-54.
- Zero-cost runs reject unknown targets instead of attempting a best effort.
- Some router conveniences cannot be used for Driver/Reviewer until they emit
  mechanically sufficient identity evidence.
- Free capacity exhaustion pauses rather than degrading across the policy
  boundary.
- Operator responsibility for credentials, network exposure, model licenses,
  upstream terms, data handling, and quotas remains explicit.

## Alternatives rejected

Replacing LiteLLM duplicates provider integration. Comparing raw strings makes
diversity cosmetic. Treating unknown cost as zero violates BTN-35. Delegating
role or cost routing to FreeLLMAPI places Battalion policy in optional,
changeable infrastructure.
