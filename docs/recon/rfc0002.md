# RFC-0002 — Battalion Knowledge Lifecycle

**Status:** Draft

---

# Summary

Engineering knowledge progresses through multiple stages.

Battalion shall treat each stage differently.

---

# Knowledge Levels

## Observation

A fact discovered during execution.

Ephemeral.

May never be stored.

---

## Candidate Instinct

Produced by Learner.

Represents a hypothesis.

Requires human review.

---

## Accepted Instinct

Human-approved engineering guidance.

Available to future executions.

Immutable.

---

## Standard

Repository documentation.

Represents established engineering practice.

Human-authored.

May cite multiple instincts.

---

## Philosophy

Rare.

Represents enduring project principles.

Examples include:

- Human-in-the-loop
- Repository-first memory
- Architectural ownership

---

# Knowledge Flow

Observation

↓

Candidate Instinct

↓

Human Review

↓

Accept

↓

Instinct Repository

↓

Relevant Node Context

Promotion to Standards is explicitly manual.

Promotion to Philosophy is explicitly manual.

---

# Design Goals

Knowledge should:

- become more durable gradually
- remain attributable
- remain reviewable
- preserve provenance
- avoid silent drift

---

# Non-goals

The Learner is not responsible for:

- deciding standards
- rewriting documentation
- modifying repository policy
